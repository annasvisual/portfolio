Customer loan Favorit

A new-generation consumer loan service with a monthly interest rate of just 4.99%. Launching in December.

Explore the case +

The need

The EU’s new Consumer Credit Directive (CCD2) brought major changes to consumer lending, creating a need to rethink the business strategy and develop a new product. Favorit was born out of this need - a loan service designed to comply with CCD2, with a strong focus on automated credit scoring through Bank iD.

The concept

As we began shaping a transparent, friendly loan service, I saw a natural fit with the orange NeedScope segment - Warm, approachable, and down-to-earth. That’s how we came up with Favorit. The name reflects our ambition to become people’s first choice for quick financing, while evoking a sense of familiarity and nostalgia through its connection to the Škoda Favorit, a discontinued model from the iconic Czech car brand. The bookmark symbol in the logo builds on that idea: a service worth saving in your browser and coming back to whenever you need it.

My contribution

I developed the concept and led the design and its implementation, working closely with our front-end developer. To make transparency tangible, I created the Contract Guide: a side-by-side view of the agreement users would sign and plain-language explanations of its key terms.

Results & impact

Scheduled to launch in December 2026, Favorit will provide a strong foundation for the company’s future CCD2-compliant products.

Investown Autoinvest Feature

Autoinvest brings Investown users a long-awaited feature: hassle-free investing tailored to their preferences.

Explore the case +

The problem

The most sought-after investment projects can be fully funded within hours, making opportunities easy to miss. Users had long requested a way to invest without constantly checking the platform, their inbox, or push notifications.

My concept

Autoinvest lets users set their investment preferences, control how much they invest, and diversify their portfolios through hassle-free automated investing. Users stay in control: they can set a maximum investment per project, keep a minimum wallet balance, reverse any automatic investment within four days, and continue investing manually.

My contribution

I created the landing page introducing Autoinvest, developed the advertising concept, and worked with a media agency to promote the feature. View landing page

The feedback

Thousands of users adopted Autoinvest. Feedback highlighted how it helped them diversify their portfolios by taking emotion out of investing, with decisions guided by predefined criteria and project data.
